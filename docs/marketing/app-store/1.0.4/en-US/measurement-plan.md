# Organic measurement plan

## Before editing App Store Connect

Fill in `baseline-template.csv` with the previous 30 days of U.S. App Store Connect data. Save screenshots of Acquisition, Metrics, Sources, Storefronts, and Devices beside the completed CSV.

Record directional U.S. storefront positions for:

- exact app name
- dating verdict
- mixed signals
- dating text
- should i text him
- situationship
- ghosting
- relationship advice
- overthinking text

Use a non-installed device or logged-out storefront where possible. Rank checks are directional; App Store Connect metrics remain the source of truth.

## Five-second comprehension gate

Test the first three screenshots with at least eight people from the target audience. Show them for five seconds, hide them, and ask:

1. What does this app do?
2. What would you give it?
3. What would it give you back?

Pass only when at least seven of eight say that the app evaluates a described dating text or situation and returns a verdict, score/evidence read, and next move. Nobody should infer screenshot upload.

## Post-release checks

Record App Store Connect results on days 3, 7, 14, and 28 using the same fields as the baseline.

- No impressions: verify availability, indexing, exact-name search, storefront, and keyword coverage.
- Impressions with very few product-page views: review the name, subtitle, and first screenshot.
- Product-page views with weak downloads: review screenshot proof, description clarity, ratings, and expectation match.
- Downloads with weak continued use: investigate the first-case experience separately from ASO.

Do not change metadata repeatedly during the first 28 days. At day 28, preserve winning fields and replace only weak keyword clusters.
