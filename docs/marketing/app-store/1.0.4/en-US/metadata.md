## Description

Mixed signals? Weird reply? Situationship making you spiral?

Describe the dating situation in a few sentences. Overthought gives you a funny Smart Verdict with a Delusion Score, evidence check, what you may be overreading, what actually matters, and one honest next move.

No screenshot uploads. No endless chatbot conversation. Just the case, the evidence, and the verdict.

Use Overthought when you are asking:
• Is he actually interested or just keeping the conversation alive?
• Am I overthinking this text?
• Should I reply, wait, ask directly, or step back?
• Is this one mixed signal—or a pattern?
• Did my ex come back with clarity or just nostalgia?
• Do I have evidence, or am I writing a whole movie?

HOW IT WORKS
1. Describe what they texted or did.
2. Get a Smart Verdict when available.
3. See your Delusion Score and evidence check.
4. Take one honest next move.
5. Save updates and track how the situation ends.

WHAT YOU GET
• Dating verdict
• Delusion Score from 0–100
• Evidence for and against your read
• What you may be overreading
• What actually matters
• One suggested next move
• Saved case history and pattern tracking
• Shareable verdict cards

Smart Verdict uses AI to create a structured read from the details you provide. Basic Verdict remains available when Smart Verdict is unavailable.

Start as a guest—no account required. Your first two guest cases include Smart Verdict. Signed-in free users receive two Smart Verdicts per day.

Overthought is entertainment and reflection, not therapy, diagnosis, crisis support, or professional dating advice. It cannot know another person’s thoughts; it evaluates the situation you describe.
