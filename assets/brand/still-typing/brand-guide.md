# Overthought | Still Typing

Approved direction: heart-shaped speech bubble with three typing dots.

## Colors
- Dusty rose: #D97791 (RGB 217, 119, 145)
- Warm cream: #F4EBDD (RGB 244, 235, 221)
- Ink: #181818 (RGB 24, 24, 24), wordmark and one-color use

## Files
- png/overthought-app-icon-1024.png: opaque square icon, cream background, no baked-in corner rounding.
- png/overthought-app-icon-2048.png: large icon export.
- png/overthought-symbol-transparent-2048.png: transparent background and transparent dot cutouts.
- png/overthought-wordmark-transparent.png: horizontal symbol and name.
- svg/: editable true vector paths, including app icon, symbol, horizontal wordmark, ink and cream variants.
- preview.png: visual overview and small-size samples.

## Typography
Wordmark: DejaVu Sans Bold, with slightly tightened spacing. Wordmark text is converted to paths in SVGs, so the font is not required to display the delivered artwork. The symbol is a vector reconstruction of the approved image; the wordmark is a typeset companion, not a claim to reproduce an identifiable font from the generated concept.

## Usage
Use the cream-backed icon as the primary app icon. Use the transparent symbol for layouts. The three dots are cutouts in transparent variants, so the background shows through. Use cream or ink variants when the rose does not contrast with a background. Leave at least one dot diameter of clear space around the symbol. Preserve proportions, three equal dots, and tail direction. Avoid gradients, glows, heavy shadows, outlines, or bright neon color substitutions.

Use the symbol without the wordmark at small sizes. Smallest supplied preview is 29px; three dots remain separated. Prefer 40px or larger where space permits.

## Handoff
Assets only. No application files, live listing, or released app have been changed. All SVGs use path geometry, not embedded raster images. PNGs are rendered from those SVGs for consistency.
